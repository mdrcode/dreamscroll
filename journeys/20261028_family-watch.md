# 2026-10-28 — Family viewing

**Status:** closed 2026-10-28

## Goal

Find something to watch with children aged 7, 4, and 2 that is not *Totoro*
(seen ~50 times) or *Bluey* (oversaturated). Constraints: mixed ages, toddler
attention span, gentle content, and enough wit that parents do not suffer.

## Corpus signals

- #264 **Minuscule** — dialogue-free French animated series about insects;
  short, visual, and unusually well suited to a 2-year-old with older siblings.
- #518 Ghibli color/visual craft; #1095 hand-painted *The Fox and the Hound*
  background; #709 *Frog and Toad* illustration.
- #566 *Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures* — action-oriented; better for the
  4/7 end than the 2-year-old.
- #532 adventurous play and preschool anxiety — supports gentle, playful
  viewing over peril-heavy plots.

## Web checks changed the list

- **Shaun the Sheep Movie**: PG, 85 min. Children and Media Australia says not
  suitable under 4, parental guidance to 7, due to scary/upsetting scenes. Drop
  it for the 2-year-old.
- **Ernest & Celestine**: PG, 80 min. Children and Media Australia flags it as
  not suitable under 6 because of scary scenes. Drop it for now.
- **The Snowman**: G, 26 min. Common Sense rates it 3+ and calls it wordless,
  kid-friendly, and brief enough for the youngest viewers. The snowman melts at
  the end; the child in the film is not distressed.
- **Puffin Rock**: TV-Y, 7 min episodes. Common Sense rates it 3+; parents
  report 2+; no violence, gentle pacing, beautiful Irish animation.
- **Room on the Broom**: 27 min. Common Sense rates it 3+; a dragon threatens
  the witch, so the under-4 set may need a lap.
- **The Gruffalo**: G, 40 min. Common Sense rates it 3+; mild peril and
  mouse-eaten visions; parents often say 4+.
- **Minuscule**: ages 0+ on TV Tantrum’s low-stimulation scale; short,
  dialogue-free insect slapstick.
- **The Red Balloon**: Common Sense rates it 7+; too old for the 2-year-old.

## Recommendation

**Push-the-envelope pick: *Minuscule*.** It avoids the Bluey/Totoro rut
completely, works visually for the 2-year-old, has real slapstick for the
7-year-old, and does not require parents to endure another song.

**Short-film pairing:** one *Minuscule* short plus *The Snowman* (26 min). That
is a complete, beautiful, wordless viewing session that feels like an event
rather than default TV.

**If the 2-year-old needs the gentlest possible thing:** *Puffin Rock*.

## Content notes

- *The Snowman*: the snowman melts; brief sadness, no violence.
- *Room on the Broom* / *The Gruffalo*: mild peril; preview with the 2-year-old.
- *Puffin Rock*: no violence; light stink-gland humor.
- *Minuscule*: insect slapstick; no dialogue.
