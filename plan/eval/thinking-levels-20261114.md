# Thinking level — ungrounded illumination, 2026-11-14

**Status:** Measured on the local corpus. Grounding is now off by default; this picks the level to
pair with it.

## Method

Everything is `gemini-3.8-flash` on the Vertex global endpoint, `capture_illumination_v2`,
`web_search = false`, `persist_projection = false` (raws only, nothing projected or linked).
104 captures per arm — the same set used by the web-search ablation, minus capture 719, whose stale
`InProgress` run refuses resubmission.

| arm | request |
| --- | --- |
| `low` | `thinking_level: "low"` |
| `medium` | omitted, which means the provider default (`medium` for this model) |
| `high` | `thinking_level: "high"` |

`minimal` is not usable here: `gemini-3.8-flash` rejects it
(`400 Thinking level is unsupported: THINKING_LEVEL_MINIMAL`), as it does `xhigh`. Supported levels
are `low`, `medium`, `high`
([docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/thinking#thinking-levels)).

Readings are in `plan/eval/illumination-readings.jsonl` across every arm ever run, including the
grounded baselines. That file is generated by `util/export-illumination-readings.py` and is not
committed. One JSON
object per reading, keyed by `capture_id` with `arm`, `model_id`, `thinking_level`, `web_search`,
`tokens`, `duration_ms`, and the parsed `summary`/`details`/`suggested_searches`/`entities`. Any
future model (Hydrogen included) lands in the same shape, so arms are joined on `capture_id`.

## The ladder

| arm | entities | zero-entity | `search_query` | out tok | thought tok | wall | thought p50 / p90 / max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `low` | 3.66 | 1 | 1.96 | 548 | **49** | **6.9 s** | 0 / 162 / 1,169 |
| `medium` | 3.66 | 2 | 2.11 | 585 | 692 | 12.1 s | 612 / 1,142 / 4,611 |
| `high` | **3.95** | 1 | **2.36** | 657 | 1,847 | 18.9 s | 1,503 / 2,496 / 11,185 |
| *(grounded baseline)* | *4.10* | *0* | *2.38* | *644* | *840* | *25.4 s* | — |

Three things fall out:

- `low` is **more than half zero-thinking** (median 0 tokens) and still lands the same entity count
  as `medium`. It is 1.75× faster and costs 14× less reasoning.
- `high` recovers most of the grounded arm's entity count (3.95 vs 4.10) and its `search_query`
  density (2.36 vs 2.38), which `medium` does not (3.66 / 2.11).
- `high` has a latency tail: mean 18.9 s but a p90 of ~2,500 thought tokens and a single capture
  (743) at **90 s / 11,185 thought tokens** — the overthinking the docs warn about.

Paired entity counts against `medium`: `low` +0.01 (t=0.10, n.s.), `high` **+0.29** (t=3.12, n=104).

## The sharpest finding: thinking level swaps a confusion

Captures 683 and 697 are two stills from *different films* that look alike — 683 is Martin Sheen in
the *Apocalypse Now* hotel room, 697 is Gary Bond in *Wake in Fright*. Same prompts, same tools:

| capture | truth | low | medium | high |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 683 | *Apocalypse Now* | **Hard to Be a God** (1989) | *Wake in Fright* | *Wake in Fright* |
| 697 | *Wake in Fright* | **Apocalypse Now** | *Wake in Fright* | *Wake in Fright* |

The answers are inverted at `low`. Whatever thinking buys here, it is not extra perception: it is
enough deliberation to land on the same (still wrong for 683) attractor. This also reinforces the
earlier conclusion that these confusions are not tool-related — 683 was misidentified with grounding
on back on 2026-10-16.

Selected as the highest entity-set spread between arms plus random picks; images re-read where a
claim was contested.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 683 hotel room | none | all three wrong; `low` invents a third film |
| 697 outback still | medium / high | `low` says *Apocalypse Now* |

| 743 39/48 sheet music | unresolved | `low` names *Alkan, 13 Prières Op. 64*; `medium` returns only "time signature"/"polymeter"; `high` frames it as a meme with no composer. I could not verify the Alkan attribution from a search, so this pair does not score for either side |

direction, not proof.

## Reading

- Thinking level is not a quality dial, it is an **answer-changing dial**: at these settings the three
  levels produce materially different entity sets on ~60% of captures.
- `low` is astonishingly cheap (0 median thought, 6.9 s) and usually good, but it is where the
  substitution errors live (van der Goes for Bouts, *Apocalypse Now* for *Wake in Fright*) and where
  type slips appear.
- `high` is the most complete and the most faithful on `search_query` density, at 2.7× the thinking
  tokens of `medium` and with a real latency tail.
- `medium` is the efficient middle: never the best in this sample, rarely the worst, half the cost of
  `high`.

## Decision (2026-11-15)

**Grounding is off by default; the thinking level stays the provider default (`medium`).** That is
the state we have measured most, and its entity count is within one of `high`'s on most captures.
`high` remains available for a targeted retry path (a capture whose illumination looks thin) rather
than as the default, because the 90-second outlier is a user-visible cost. `low` is not the default:
it is genuinely cheaper, but the substitution errors it makes are exactly the kind that poison
identity resolution. The trade-off is recorded in `plan/pragmatism.md`.
