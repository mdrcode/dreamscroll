# Gemini 4 Hydrogen vs Flash 3.8 — illumination quality and price, 2026-11-14

**Status:** measured on the local corpus. Recommendation at the end.

## Method

Same 104 captures, same prompt (`capture_illumination_v2`), same endpoint, `web_search = false` (our
new default), `persist_projection = false` (raws only, nothing projected or linked). Only the model and
thinking level vary.

| cell | request |
| --- | --- |
| **Flash 3.8 medium** (current prod flow) | `gemini-3.8-flash`, level omitted → provider default `medium` |
| Hydrogen low | `gemini-4-hydrogen`, `thinking_level: "low"` |
| Hydrogen medium | `gemini-4-hydrogen`, `thinking_level: "medium"` |

Hydrogen needs no extra header or body change on our Interactions client; `gemini-4-hydrogen` was
overridden by environment for these runs, so the repo config still points at Flash. Readings land in
`plan/eval/illumination-readings.jsonl` (generated with `util/export-illumination-readings.py`, not
committed) with `model_id` + `thinking_level`, so cells are joins on `capture_id`.

## Price — at a glance

List prices, standard tier, USD per 1M tokens, as of 2026-11-15. We call Vertex, so treat these as
close-but-confirm-against-the-bill.

| model | input $/1M | output $/1M (thinking billed here) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flash 3.8 | **0.75** now → **1.50** from 2027-01-01 | **3.75** now → **7.50** from 2027-01-01 |

Flash 3.8's promo runs through 2026-12-31; after that it is priced identically to Hydrogen. The two
regimes are shown separately below because they give different answers.

| cell | input tok | billed output tok | cost **now** | cost **from 2027-01-01** | **$/1,000 now** | **$/1,000 later** |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| Hydrogen / low | 329,321 | 124,192 | — | **$1.425** | — | **$14.25** |
| Hydrogen / medium | 329,321 | 198,866 | — | **$1.985** | — | **$19.85** |

"Billed output" is `output + thinking`, since thinking tokens are charged at the output rate and the

- **Today Flash is 1.9–2.7× cheaper** than Hydrogen low–medium.
- **From 2027-01-01 the price argument mostly disappears**, and one row flips: Flash medium
  ($14.87/1k) becomes marginally *more* expensive than Hydrogen low ($14.25/1k), because Flash
  medium spends more billed output (132,675 vs 124,192) and the rates are then equal.
- The input half is unavoidable: Hydrogen doubles the rate on the ~3,160 tokens per capture that both
  models spend, and tokenizes our images marginally larger (+16 tok/capture over 104 runs).

## Grounding — the cost we stopped paying

Vertex bills grounding **per query**, not per request: **$14 per 1,000 grounding queries**, first
**5,000 queries per month free** (shared across Gemini 3 and 4), and *input tokens supplied by
grounding are not charged*. A request may issue several queries.

How often does an illumination actually search? Two probes:

| probe | sample | fired | queries per illumination |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| prompt + image, **no response schema** | 14 | 3 | 0.36 |
| prompt + image + **real schema**, through the app | 12 | 2 | **0.75** |

The schema-faithful number is the one to use. Its distribution is the point: 10 captures fired zero
queries, one fired one, and **capture 743 (a sheet-music page) fired eight queries in a single call** —
2.4× the latency and $0.112 of grounding for that capture alone, 16× its own token cost. Search is
triggered by *our inability to read the image from prior knowledge*: recognisable stills and covers go
unsearched; sheet music, papers, and obscure citations get hunted.

### Illustrative grounding cost per 1,000 captures

Grounded Flash 3.8 medium tokens run **$7.93/1k** (vs $7.43/1k ungrounded), so:

| queries/capture | grounding $/1k | total $/1k | vs ungrounded |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 — free tier, or nothing fires | $0.00 | **$7.93** | +7% |
| 0.36 — schema-free lower bound | $5.04 | $12.97 | +75% |
| **0.75 — measured with schema** | **$10.50** | **$18.43** | **+148%** |
| 1 — every capture fires once | $14.00 | $21.93 | +195% |
| 2 | $28.00 | $35.93 | +384% |

At the measured rate, grounding **more than doubled** the token cost of the prod flow. A backfill pays
this on the whole corpus at once, which is why it is the operation that surfaces the fee.

### Recovering the October backfill spend

No API can return it: Cloud Billing exposes accounts, budgets and a price catalog, but historical
costs only exist if **billing export to BigQuery** is enabled, and project `mdrcode` has no datasets
(`bigquery/v2/projects/mdrcode/datasets` → empty). So the October figure is not recoverable from the
API as configured.

It can be *inverted* from the invoice instead. With `q` queries per capture, `N` captures cost
`N × q × $0.014`:

| backfill size | $10 | $25 | $50 | $100 | $200 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 500 | 1.4 | 3.6 | 7.1 | 14.3 | 28.6 |
| 1,000 | 0.7 | 1.8 | 3.6 | 7.1 | 14.3 |
| 2,000 | 0.4 | 0.9 | 1.8 | 3.6 | 7.1 |
| 5,000 | 0.1 | 0.4 | 0.7 | 1.4 | 2.9 |
| 10,000 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.7 | 1.4 |

Cells are the implied queries per capture. A fee that implies >1 query per capture means the
backfill's corpus is more search-prone than this one — plausible, since the material that triggers
search (dense text, citations, sheet music) is exactly what people screenshot.

Going forward this is auditable without billing access: grounded runs now record
`provider_metadata.provider_search_queries` (`src/llms/gemini/client.rs`), so the billed query count
per run is in `inference_metas` and a backfill's total is one `sum`.

## What the outputs look like

| cell | entities/capture | zero-entity captures | `search_query`/capture |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Flash 3.8 / medium | 3.66 | 2 | 2.12 |
| Hydrogen / low | 3.38 | 8 | 1.72 |
| Hydrogen / medium | 4.14 | 10 | 2.30 |

Entity sets diverge from Flash on most captures (Jaccard 0.54 low, 0.52 medium; ~26 of 104 identical).
Hydrogen is **bimodal**: it either emits a longer list than Flash (740: 15 books and authors vs 4
series-level entities; 714: 8 chart entries vs 5) or emits nothing at all (10 captures at medium).

Zero entities does **not** mean it did not know. Capture 754's Hydrogen output names "standard diving
dress" and "manual air pump" in prose while claiming no entities; 758's names "Googie" and "Space Age
Bachelor Pad". The knowledge is there; the emission is not. That distinction matters for us because the
graph consumes `entities`, not prose.

## Recommendation

1. **Keep Flash 3.8 / medium as the production flow.** It is 1.9–2.7× cheaper *today*, faster than
   Hydrogen medium, and emits a more complete entity list for the ordinary capture.
2. **Do not adopt Hydrogen as the default** on the current price sheet. Hydrogen medium is the only
   cell that beats Flash on entity count (4.14 vs 3.66) and it costs 2.7×; Hydrogen low costs 1.9×
   and returns *fewer* entities. The picture weakens once Flash's promo ends — see caveat 2.
3. **Hydrogen is attractive as a targeted retry.** Its wins are concentrated on hard visual
   identification (708) and text-dense images (740, 714) — exactly the captures where Flash returns
   zero or thin entities. A retry path that calls Hydrogen low only when Flash produces ≤1 entity
   would touch a few percent of captures, cost cents, and buy the 708-class wins. That needs a task
   model override (admin-gated, since a client-chosen model is a spend vector).
4. **Before any of that, fix the emission question.** Ten of 104 Hydrogen medium runs returned no
   entities while describing the subject accurately in prose. If a small prompt nudge ("emit every
   entity you can name") closes that, Hydrogen's profile changes and this price comparison should be
   re-run.

## Caveats

- Prices are Developer API list; our Vertex bill may differ. Flash 3.8's promo expires 2026-12-31: at
  the standard rates Flash medium ($14.87/1k) is marginally *pricier* than Hydrogen low ($14.25/1k) on
  this workload, so from that date the choice is behaviour-only and should be re-decided, not assumed.
- Judgement is 14 captures, unblinded, no CI. Entity-set metrics are mechanical but naming variance
  counts against both arms equally.
- Capture 719 is excluded across all arms (a stale `InProgress` run refuses resubmission).
